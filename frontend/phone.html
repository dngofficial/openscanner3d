Guided scan

  1. Put the object on a table with space to walk around it. It must not move during the scan.
  2. Stand at the side you want as the front and point the phone at the object.
  3. Tap Start and allow the camera and motion access.
  4. Keep the object inside the dashed circle. Follow the instructions at the top; photos are taken automatically when you reach a missing angle and hold still.

Keep the phone the same way up (portrait or landscape) for the whole scan.
