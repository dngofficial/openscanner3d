Reconstruction settings

Removes the stationary background from model inputs. Use a plain backdrop and keep hands out of each photo. Disable this when moving the camera around a stationary scene. Automatic passes use at most 8 views and wait at least 8 seconds between starts. Finish uses the selected final budget, sampled across the entire recording.
Limits and capture tips

This is periodic neural reconstruction, not video-rate SLAM. Capture and frame selection continue during background inference. Point-cloud updates arrive whenever a pass finishes.

For manual capture, rotate the item in small steps with overlapping surface detail. Stop moving and remove your hands before each photo. One photo cannot show the hidden sides.

The model estimates shape and cameras. Scale is arbitrary; unseen parts, shiny surfaces and weak overlap remain difficult. PLY contains colored points, not a watertight surface mesh.

For an 8 GB GPU, start with 224 pixels and 3–4 views per pass. A larger photo set is sampled to your view budget; increase it only if memory permits. Existing successful results are preserved after failures.
